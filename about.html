About

VP

I'm Vikram Puar, a biomedical engineering student at Boston University (Class of 2027), originally from Pebble Beach, California.

Since May 2025 I've been an undergraduate research assistant in BU's Neurovascular Imaging Laboratory, where I received a grant for research on in vivo oxygen delivery in Alzheimer's mice during sleep. I also develop MATLAB analysis code and automated our data analysis, cutting processing time from 1 hour to under 15 minutes.

In spring 2026 I was an operations and engineering intern at Houghton Chemical, doing quality control analysis and competitor research. That summer I worked as a research technician at Active Site in Cambridge, running cell culture, PCR, and transfection workflows and designing primers for cloning and mutagenesis.

This year I'm a teaching assistant for Intro to Engineering, helping students with CAD, 3D printing, and Arduino, and I'm working with my senior design team on an autoinjector. Outside of engineering I compete in tournaments with the BU Club Golf team (single-digit handicap), and I served as VP of Engagement for Kappa Theta Pi, planning networking events for up to 120 students and alumni.
